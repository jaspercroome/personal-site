I've written before about software factories and how they're the wrong form factor for most dev shops. If you're focused on throughput, that's great. But look around you, my brothers and sisters - the slopocalypse is here. Nobody needs MOAR FUNCTIONS, we need more ambition and more control.  

This idea came back to the forefront for me w/ fable 5.1 and astra 6, both of which are world-changing models (again, I'm tired boss). Grappling with how to best leverage these models left me with a heavy dose of AI Psychosis, but 
Software factories aren't wrong, they're incomplete.

## amibtions are the whole game
Back in July, @thariq went onstage at AIE WF and gave a banger of a speech titled [Field Guide to Fable](https://ai.engineer/talks/9fubhllmsBU-field-guide-to-fable). In it, he speaks about how he works with this latest batch of frontier models, specifically Fable 5. The line of his that really stuck with me from this presentation is to 'Be Unreasonable'. This is such a gem of a line, and it's going to age very, very well for 2 distinct reasons. 


**Reason #1** is that these models are _reasoning_ models. They're going to continue to eat away at tasks in software that demand reasonable mindsets. Bug fixes, developing a feature to spec, version updates, back testing - all of these pieces require reason. They won't discover novel ideas without getting nudged in the right direction, which brings me to #2


**Reason #2** is that work requiring a _lack of reason_ is what we get to play with. Product evolution, setting new performance standards, remixing this tool over here with that idea over there. This is the fun, inventive, creative space we get to occupy now. We ~~get~~ have to be ambitious! It's not just something we _can_ do, it's the whole game now.
